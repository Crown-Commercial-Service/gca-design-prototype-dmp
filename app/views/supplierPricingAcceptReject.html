1 To provide realistic, reasonable direct labour rates that reflect the salaries required to attract and retain the necessary talent, Leidos bases our direct labour rate estimates on a defined hierarchy of methods. We use actual salaries or letters of intent for key personnel and named personnel when we have a high degree of certainty, at the time of proposal estimating, that such known or named personnel will execute the proposed contract work. We use forward pricing labour rates (FPLRs) for unnamed personnel. These consistent methodologies for proposing direct labour enable Leidos to provide reasonable, realistic estimates that we base on actual salary data for Leidos personnel. When assessing the costs associated with varying terms, the direct labour costs assume current economic conditions and the impact of inflation as a compounded rate based on the current ONS CPI or the most recent CPI Forecast Evaluation Report from the Office of Budget Responsibility.
